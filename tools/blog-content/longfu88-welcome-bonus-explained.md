## Turnover and Wagering Basics

Bonus funds are not the same as cash you deposit. Before a welcome bonus (or the winnings generated from it) can be withdrawn, it almost always has to be played through a set number of times — a **turnover** or **wagering requirement**, often written as a multiplier like "10x" or "20x" of the bonus amount, or sometimes the deposit-plus-bonus combined. This is standard across the online casino industry, not something unique to Longfu88.

The specific multiplier that applies to the 188% welcome bonus isn't stated in the terms we've verified, so we're not going to guess at a number here. What we can tell you honestly: read the turnover figure shown on the offer itself before you opt in, and don't assume it matches a number you've seen quoted for a different promotion or a different operator.
