## What Live Casino Actually Means

Live casino is the middle ground between a physical casino floor and a fully automated RNG slot. A real dealer shuffles a real shoe or rolls real dice inside a broadcast studio, a camera streams the action to your device, and your bets are placed through the Longfu88 interface rather than at a rail. You're seeing the same shuffle, the same deal and the same reveal as anyone else watching that table — nothing about the outcome is generated by software the way a slot's spin is. The trade-off is pace: a live table moves at the speed of a real dealer, not the speed of an animation, which is exactly why some players prefer it and others gravitate toward faster formats instead.

## Live Casino vs. P2P: A Quick Distinction

Two live tables in the lobby share names with titles found elsewhere on the site — baccarat and dragon tiger both also exist as **P2P (player-vs-player)** titles, which is where confusion sometimes creeps in. The difference is structural, not cosmetic: in live casino, you're betting against the house's fixed odds on an outcome revealed by a real dealer on a video stream, and every player at that table is betting independently against the same house line. In P2P, there's no dealer representing the house at all — your result depends on how your hand compares against other real players in that round. Same name, genuinely different product. Worth knowing before you assume a "dragon tiger" table in one section works the same way as one in the other.
