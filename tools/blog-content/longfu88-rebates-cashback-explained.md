## Rebate vs. Cashback

Rebates and cashback both return a portion of your play back to you, but they work differently, and Longfu88 runs both. A **rebate** is a small, ongoing percentage credited on your turnover across a game vertical, paid regardless of whether you won or lost that session — it's closer to a loyalty mechanic than a promotion you opt into each time. **Cashback**, by contrast, is typically framed around losses over a defined period (a weekend, a week) and comes with a stated ceiling on the maximum payout. Both are worth understanding on their own terms rather than lumping them together as "money back," since the mechanics and caps differ.

The distinction matters practically, too. Because rebates have no turnover requirement of their own — they're a straight percentage credited on qualifying play — there's nothing further to "clear" before a rebate is usable. Cashback, in most industry structures, can sometimes carry a smaller playthrough requirement of its own before it's withdrawable, though Longfu88's specific handling of this for its cashback offers isn't detailed in what we've verified. Check the terms on the specific cashback offer before assuming it behaves exactly like a rebate.
