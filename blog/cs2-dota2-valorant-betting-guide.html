CS2, Dota 2 & Valorant Betting Guide (Malaysia)

By Lucas Chong · Last updated 2026-07-16

18+ · Play responsibly. Help: Befrienders Malaysia · Talian Kasih 15999.

CS2, Dota 2 and Valorant are three of the biggest titles in competitive esports, and Malaysian bettors who already follow the region's MLBB scene are increasingly branching into all three. They all support similar bet types on paper — match winner, map handicap, totals — but the way each game actually plays out changes what those markets feel like to bet on. A round-based shooter and a long-form strategy MOBA reward different kinds of attention from a bettor. This guide walks through what distinguishes the three titles for betting purposes, the common markets across all of them, and a worked example in ringgit, using Longfu88's esports betting on the Sportsbook as the reference point.

The Three Titles at a Glance

  • Counter-Strike 2 (CS2) — a tactical first-person shooter played in rounds, where two teams of five alternate attacking and defending sides across a map, with the first team to a set number of round wins taking that map. One of the longest-running esports titles, with a deep and consistent global competitive scene.
  • Dota 2 — a PC MOBA (multiplayer online battle arena) built around hero drafting, item economy and objective control across a single long-form match per game, typically bundled into a best-of-2 or best-of-3 series. Matches run longer than a CS2 map and reward understanding of team compositions and strategic pacing.
  • Valorant — a round-based tactical shooter that layers character abilities on top of Counter-Strike-style gunplay, with a structure similar to CS2 (rounds building to a map win) but its own agent-pick and ability layer shaping strategy.

Common Markets Per Title

  • Map winner — a bet on which team wins a specific map, available across all three titles in largely the same form.
  • Map or round handicap — a virtual head start or deficit applied to level a mismatched series (map-level for series structure, round-level within a single CS2 or Valorant map), settling on the same push / half-win / half-loss logic covered in our Asian Handicap guide.
  • Totals (over/under) — a bet on the total maps in a series, or the total rounds within an individual CS2 or Valorant map, depending on how the market is structured.
  • Pistol round winner (CS2 and Valorant) — a bet on which side wins the first round of a half, played with cheaper starting equipment; a genuinely different, lower-information market compared to a full-map bet since it happens before either team has built an economy.
  • First blood / early objective props (Dota 2) — generic markets tied to which side secures an early kill or objective (such as the first Roshan kill) rather than the match outcome itself. Exact prop naming and availability vary by sportsbook.

How the Titles Differ for Bettors

CS2 and Valorant share a round-based rhythm that makes them relatively easy to follow live — a round ends every couple of minutes, so a map-handicap or round-totals bet resolves with a steady stream of feedback while the match is underway. Dota 2 plays out over a single longer match with fewer discrete "round" checkpoints, so its markets tend to reward bettors who understand the game's drafting phase and mid-match economy swings rather than those simply watching for the next scoreboard tick. None of that makes one title inherently more or less predictable than another — it changes what kind of attention pays off. A bettor who's watched a lot of CS2 pistol rounds develops a feel for how cheap-equipment rounds tend to play out; a bettor who follows Dota 2 closely develops a feel for which drafts tend to snowball versus which tend to fall behind early and recover late. Neither pattern-reading skill transfers cleanly to the other title.

Odds and Bankroll: A Worked MYR Example

Odds across all three titles are typically shown in decimal format on the Longfu88 Sportsbook, the same style used for football and other sports — the number is your total return per ringgit staked, stake included. Say a CS2 map-handicap market shows illustrative odds of 1.90 on a team at a -1.5 round handicap within a single map (a hypothetical price for teaching purposes, not a quote tied to any real match). Stake MYR 80 on that line and the team wins the map by 2 or more rounds, clearing -1.5: you collect MYR 80 × 1.90 = MYR 152, for MYR 72 profit. Now compare that to a Dota 2 match-winner bet at illustrative odds of 1.50 on a heavier favourite — the same MYR 80 stake returns MYR 80 × 1.50 = MYR 120, just MYR 40 profit, reflecting the shorter price on a more likely outcome. Neither bet is inherently "better" — a shorter price on a stronger favourite and a longer price on a tighter handicap line are just different risk-reward trade-offs, and sizing your stake consistently across both (rather than betting bigger purely because a price looks generous) is the more disciplined approach.

Responsible Betting

All three titles can move quickly once a match is live — a round loss in CS2 or Valorant, or a bad early Dota 2 fight, can tempt a chase bet on the next line rather than sticking to your original plan. Decide your stake and your session limit before a match starts, not partway through it, and treat any title-specific pattern-reading as informed context rather than a guarantee — even a well-understood matchup can go the other way. Common Questions

Which title is easiest for beginners: CS2, Dota 2 or Valorant?＋

CS2 and Valorant tend to be the more approachable starting point for new bettors, since their round-based structure and shorter, more frequent objectives are easier to follow live than Dota 2's longer, more strategically layered matches. Dota 2 rewards bettors who already understand its drafting and item-economy systems, so it's worth watching a few matches before betting on it specifically.

What is a map handicap?＋

A map handicap gives one side a virtual map or round head start (or deficit) to level a mismatched series before it starts, the same logic as an Asian Handicap in football but applied to maps or rounds instead of goals. It can produce a push, half-win or half-loss on finer lines, the same way a football handicap does.

What are the big events in these titles?＋

Each title has its own marquee annual or seasonal event — Dota 2's The International, CS2's Major championships, and Valorant's Champions — bringing together the top teams globally. We don't reference specific dates, brackets or past results here since those change every year; check each title's official channels or the sportsbook's fixture list for current scheduling.

Is in-play betting available for CS2, Dota 2 and Valorant?＋

Sportsbooks that carry these titles often extend markets into live play once a match starts, with round or map score lines updating in real time. Whether in-play is available for a specific fixture depends on the match and the sportsbook at the time, so check the live betting section directly rather than assuming it's always available.
