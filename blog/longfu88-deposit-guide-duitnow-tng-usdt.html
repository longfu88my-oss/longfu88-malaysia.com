Troubleshooting a Stuck Deposit

If you've sent a deposit and it isn't reflected in your balance:

  • Give it a few minutes first. Not every method credits instantly, particularly crypto deposits waiting on network confirmations.
  • Check your payment confirmation. Confirm the transaction actually completed on the DuitNow, Touch 'n Go, Visa or wallet side — a failed or pending transaction on your end won't show up on Longfu88's side at all.
  • For crypto deposits, double-check the network and address you sent to matched exactly what was shown in your Longfu88 cashier at the time.
  • Keep your reference number or transaction receipt. If the deposit genuinely hasn't credited after a reasonable wait, contact 24/7 live chat or support@longfu88.com with that reference ready — it's the fastest way for support to trace the transaction.
  • Don't send the deposit a second time while troubleshooting a missing first one; resolve the original transaction before attempting another.

Worth noting: a deposit that's stuck is not the same thing as a deposit that's lost. E-wallet and bank-linked transfers almost always resolve one way or the other (credited, or bounced back to your own account) rather than disappearing, and crypto transactions are traceable on the network itself, which is exactly why keeping your reference and transaction hash matters — it gives support something concrete to check rather than a general complaint to investigate from scratch.
