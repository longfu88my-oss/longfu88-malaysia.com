The basics

What Fishing Games Are & How They Play

Fishing games — also called fish shooting games — are an arcade genre built around a single, satisfying loop: a cannon, a screen full of moving targets, and the choice of when and where to fire. You control the cannon at the bottom of the screen, pick how much each bullet costs, and shoot at the fish drifting past. Land enough hits on a creature and it bursts, paying out based on its value. Small fish go down with a single shot; the big bosses take a barrage and pay the most.

What makes the genre so addictive is the blend of skill and chance. You decide which targets to chase, how heavily to fire and when to hold back — but the multipliers and special weapons that drop are down to luck. Most rooms layer in bonus features like lightning chains, bombs and lock-on cannons that clear the screen, keeping every session unpredictable. There is no spin button to wait on; the action is continuous, and that is exactly why so many Malaysian players keep coming back.

Because the rules are visual and instant, fishing games are some of the easiest casino titles to pick up. You do not need to learn paylines or table rules — you can see exactly what you are shooting at and what it is worth. Set your bullet cost to match your budget and you are away.
